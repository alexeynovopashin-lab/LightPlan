Two fixes, and the data cannot say which one is meant: pass `"name": name(p)` in Swift (the point is already known in `.badSky(let p, _)`), or drop `{name}` from the four texts to match ru. No fix proposed in `lang_check_proposed_fixes.json`.
- `week.wx` — zh only. Swift (`PlannerWeekView.swift`) passes `cloud` and `wind`, so the zh week row would show a literal «{cond}». ru, en, es and ja have no `{cond}`. A fix is in `lang_check_proposed_fixes.json` (drop `{cond}·`). The key comes from the web's `lang.js`, and `lang_native.json` cannot carry a key the web has (`lang2xcstrings.js` refuses it), so the word is changed on the web side and brought in by `make lang`.
